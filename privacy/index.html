Privacy Policy

Your privacy and operational security are central to our commitments as your logistics partner.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Skylink Corridor ("Skylink Corridors", "we", "us", or "our") operates integrated transportation, warehousing, cross-border freight, and supply chain logistics services. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards and practices regarding the collection, handling, storage, and protection of information obtained through our website, digital booking systems, quote requests, customer portals, and day-to-day operations.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate freight estimates, shipment execution, and customer support, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, professional title, corporate affiliation, business email address, physical address, and telephone numbers.
  • Shipment & Logistics Data: Origin and destination addresses, consignee details, cargo specifications, weight, volume, commodity classifications, customs documents, and delivery instructions.
  • Operational & Tracking Information: Milestone timestamps, GPS coordinates, proof-of-delivery signatures, vehicle telemetry, and exception logs.
  • Technical & Website Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and page interaction records gathered through essential cookies and server logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling quotations, rate calculations, and contract preparation.
  • Executing scheduled domestic transportation, warehousing, and cross-border transit.
  • Facilitating customs clearance, port coordination, and regulatory compliance.
  • Providing real-time shipment milestone notifications and proof-of-delivery documentation.
  • Maintaining network security, fraud prevention, and operational safety.
  • Responding to customer inquiries, complaints, and service adjustments.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data. Information may only be disclosed under the following controlled circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Vetted carriers, customs brokerage partners, port terminal operators, and IT infrastructure providers directly assisting in shipment fulfillment.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Government agencies, customs authorities, and law enforcement when required by statutory obligations or valid legal processes.
  • Corporate Transactions: In the event of an operational reorganization, merger, or asset transfer, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard organizational, administrative, and technical measures to protect all customer and operational information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Shipment records are retained for the duration required to fulfill contractual obligations, resolve claims, and satisfy statutory tax and commercial archiving laws.
